## Judgment: V8 Versus V7

**Yes, V8 improves focal hierarchy, but does not reach the cinematic art gate.**
The blue envelope isolates Core from the diagonal galaxy and creates the frame's
main luminous region. In V7, the small white sphere and thin scattered loops
compete more with Earth's large bright surface and Learning's pale cap. In V8,
Core attracts attention first more reliably, although Earth and Fitness still
carry much larger visible surface areas.

The tradeoff is clear: V8's fewer, brighter bundles form tidy petal-like ellipses
around the sphere, making the atomic-emblem reading stronger than V7's sprawling
strands. Its broad cyan haze improves emphasis more than dimensionality. The
reference connects an irregular central emitter to space through selective
filaments, interrupted dust and localized light, while retaining deep shadows.
V8 still reads predominantly as separately lit objects on a starfield.

## Ranked Art Gaps

1. **Lighting and atmosphere do not yet bind the scene.** Core sits in a broad,
   soft blue patch, but the adjacent space has little directional light structure.
   Calendar has a broad pale patch on its face, Learning a conspicuous icy top
   cap, Home broad gold/silver reflections, and the bottom-right foreground body
   a large pale blue surface. These highlights offer competing destinations.
   The reference gives Calendar and Fitness dark faces with selective bright
   edges, Home a warm lit hemisphere, and Core the richest luminous surroundings.
   The render does not establish which rig or shader choices caused the gap.
2. **Depth is present but weakly articulated.** Lower-left rocks and the cropped
   lower-right body already supply foreground silhouettes; they are not absent.
   However, the rocks stay largely at the edge, look similarly sharp to the
   planets, and do little to overlap the system. Large blue orbit ellipses trace
   almost complete, evenly readable outlines around empty space. The reference
   uses a receding, overlapping asteroid band and interrupted orbital arcs to
   carry the eye between depth layers. A diagonal galaxy alone cannot do that.
3. **Core still looks constructed rather than stellar.** The V8 close-up shows
   real blue-white granular detail, not a blank white disk. But that fine pattern
   is nearly uniform across the face; there is little large-scale tonal structure
   or separation between photosphere and limb. Several thin parallel strands
   follow almost identical elliptical paths, with a bright narrow spike above
   right. Organic strands are already primary-owned work; judge their eventual
   result in the whole frame, not only enlarged.
4. **Home has construction, but insufficient material storytelling.** The V7
   close-up visibly contains thick beveled plates, recessed black joints, fine
   grain and a few unequal panel shapes. The remaining problem is the repeated
   large flat polygon faces and similar edge treatment: it reads as a bronze
   faceted shell. The upper-left white reflection and broad central gold patches
   overpower subtler construction. The reference's broken interlocking plates,
   varied relief and dark exposed areas give a stronger assembled-world reading.
5. **Secondary bodies remain diagrammatic in specific ways.** Learning's bright
   trunk has sparse, nearly straight diagonal branches with little projected
   overlap; the top highlight competes with their tips. Earth's tan/gray land
   and white streaks dominate its face, with less readable blue ocean than the
   reference. Fitness has a convincing equatorial shaft and visible plate stacks,
   but its broad blue/brown surface mottling is softer and less structurally
   varied than the reference's mineral relief. These are whole-frame observations;
   no new Learning, Earth or Fitness close-ups were supplied here.

## Strongest Two Next Changes

1. **Art-direct shared lighting and a source-bound atmospheric envelope.** Keep
   V8's central emphasis, but break the smooth cyan patch into a compact bright
   region and sparse, uneven illuminated dust with dark gaps. Reveal this through
   selective scattering and reflections around Core. Reduce the competing broad
   pale caps/reflections on Learning, Calendar and the foreground body; retain
   Home's warm identity and dark hemispheres elsewhere. Acceptance: at thumbnail
   size Core leads immediately, and at full size local light reveals surface form
   without flattening the planets or turning the center into a fog disk. This
   complements the pending strand work rather than redesigning its geometry.
2. **Stage a convincing foreground-to-distance progression.** Reposition a small
   number of foreground rocks for deliberate peripheral overlap, vary silhouette
   and apparent size along a receding band, and soften only the nearest layer.
   Let orbit arcs fade and disappear with depth/occlusion, with a few selective
   highlights rather than complete equal-strength ellipses. Keep the galaxy
   subordinate. Acceptance: foreground, planetary system and distant environment
   read as three distinct layers even with labels hidden and motion stopped.
   Assess this in the primary's forthcoming portrait composition as well as the
   landscape frame; do not create a competing portrait layout.
