## Progress and Tradeoffs

Core's open, tapered, irregular streamers replace the earlier closed ribbon reading.
The softer scattering now contains structure and dark gaps. At landscape size,
the pale textured sphere feels less emissive than the reference's radiant
center; several thin streamers disappear against the galaxy. Core remains the main
blue-white luminous region, but its light does little to connect nearby surfaces.

Learning's large white cap and colored reflection blobs are gone in the V17 frames.
Branches and nodes read cleanly through the dark shell. The tradeoff is a thick,
fairly continuous violet rim that makes the shell look heavier and the tree flatter
than the reference's luminous interior.

Home now has visibly recessed module pockets, unequal relief and varied surface
response. The isolated front shows deep recesses on the left/lower face; V17's
landscape retains a visible dark pocket and warm/cool plate contrast. These are
real construction gains. Fine chipped detail is not reliably readable at whole-frame
scale; the remaining tiled impression does not justify repeating the large-panel diagnosis.

Fitness is materially better: mineral relief survives on a darker hemisphere,
and the metal stacks/shaft retain selective highlights without the previous broad
blue fill. Preserve that shadow budget. The lower-right foreground body's pale
reflection is also reduced. The curved debris trail now recedes in apparent size;
its near-black, separated silhouettes still provide little illuminated depth rhythm.

Portrait keeps all identities visible and makes Core's light reach Calendar/Home
more convincingly. But the system occupies a compact middle band with roughly a
third of the image empty above and below; foreground depth cues are absent there.
Landscape retains a spacious but disconnected arrangement. These framing gaps
remain visible independently of labels or application UI.

1. **Finish the shared light hierarchy around the existing Core.** Add a compact
   blue-white surface/limb emission peak, retaining darker granular detail; strengthen
   only selected streamer roots and sparse nearby dust. Put restrained Core-facing
   accents on neighboring surfaces/debris, with shadows between them. Break Learning's
   continuous rim into selective highlights; keep Home warm and Fitness dark/mineral.
   Acceptance: at thumbnail size Core leads immediately in both frames; at full size
   its brightest surface, streamer roots and nearby accents read as one light source.
   No flat white disk, renewed reflection blobs or uniformly bright secondary rims.
2. **Compose depth deliberately for each aspect ratio.** In landscape, turn the
   existing debris curve into a legible progression using sparse grazing highlights,
   varied spacing and softer nearest rocks. In portrait, expand/reposition the system
   to use more vertical space and retain a peripheral foreground/debris cue; protect
   clearance around Earth/Fitness and between Core, Calendar and Home.
   Acceptance: both authored frames show foreground, system and distant sky at rest;
   portrait reads as an intentional full composition rather than a small central group
