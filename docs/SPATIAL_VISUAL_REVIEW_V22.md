## Improvements and Remaining Tradeoffs

Core now leads more decisively in both frames. The stronger blue-white surface
retains granular detail; portrait no longer depends mainly on the cyan envelope
for emphasis. Open streamers and uneven scattering remain successful. Further
blanket brightening is not the highest-value next step: the reference distributes
that energy through nearby filaments, dust and selective surface accents.

Learning's curved, overlapping branch tiers and nodes give more internal depth
than V17's frontal tree. V22's darker directional shell reduces the continuous
heavy violet annulus; portrait has a localized cool upper-right highlight rather
than the old broad reflection blobs. Landscape's smallest branchlets are subdued,
but the Learning identity survives. The earlier close-up does not prove rig parity.

Calendar's inward ticks now read as deliberate time markings instead of a fuzzy
outer edge. In V22, the broad pale specular spots visible in V21's close-up are
absent; its satin cloudy surface sits behind the luminous ring and cyan marker.
Reduced fills also keep Fitness's mineral hemisphere dark and suppress the large
foreground reflection. Home retains warm plates, recessed pockets and cool accents.

The darker treatment trades some secondary readability for hierarchy: Earth's
ocean/land color distinction remains weak, and debris is mostly near-black silhouettes.
These limitations do not establish a new object-geometry regression.

Portrait improves substantially on V17's small middle cluster: the system uses
more height with clear identities and a central Core. The tradeoff is separated
tiers: Learning floats above Calendar/Home, and a broad dark gap separates Core
from Earth/Fitness. No foreground debris anchors that frame. Landscape still has
depth silhouettes, but lacks the reference's illuminated, receding connective band.

   Keep dark gaps, softened nearest rocks and subordinate orbit lines.
   Acceptance: at whole-frame size the eye follows a readable foreground-to-system
   path; accents plausibly share Core's source, and Core remains the brightest focus.

   and bring Earth/Fitness closer to Core's outer energy, preserving silhouettes

   Acceptance: portrait reads as one connected spatial group with near/middle/far
